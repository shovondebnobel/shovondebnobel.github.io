Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Privacy is taken into account when this website is being designed. The amount of information processed is limited solely to the purpose of running the site and responding to visitors.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, lnc. GitHub may automatically collect and store server logs from your browser. According to the GitHub Privacy Statement, this data includes visitor IP address, the user agent string of your browser, and the dates and times of visits. This data may be transferred to and processed by GitHub, Inc. in the United States.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is employed in order to get a basic understanding of anonymous website traffic and it does not make use of cookies or gather any personal information such as your name, email address, or IP address.

Contact Form

The details you provide when using the contact form, such as your name, email address, and message, are sent to the site owner through StaticForms (staticforms.dev) and are used solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

Third-Party Services

This site could load resources from third-party services, such as Font Awesome, and include links to external scripts, profiles; the privacy policies of those respective services come into effect when you use them.

Cookies

The site does not employ cookies for the purpose of tracking.

Updates

The policy could be revised if there are changes to the website or its services.
